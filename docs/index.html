Reset the service reminder on your Triumph Tiger 900.

The app connects your phone to the motorcycle through a vLinker MC+ adapter. It resets the service date and distance, reads fault codes, and clears them when you choose.

No account, subscription, advertising, dealer visit, or ECU tuning.

Android installation iPhone installation

What is it for?

After you service the motorcycle yourself or use an independent workshop, the dashboard can continue showing the service warning. Reset Moto Reminders lets you finish that job without paying a dealer or buying an annual diagnostic-app subscription.

It also reads engine diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), explains known codes in plain language, and can clear stored codes after you have read them. Clearing a code does not repair the fault that caused it.

What you need

  • An Android phone or iPhone.
  • A vLinker MC+ Bluetooth OBD adapter.
  • The correct diagnostic-port cable for your motorcycle, if required.

How to use it

  1. Connect the adapter

    Plug the vLinker MC+ into the motorcycle diagnostic port. Turn the ignition on and leave the engine off.

  2. Connect the phone

    On Android, pair vLinker MC-Android in Bluetooth settings with PIN 1234. On iPhone, connect to vLinker MC-IOS from inside the app.

  3. Read the motorcycle first

    Tap Connect, then Read motorcycle. Check that the displayed odometer is plausible before doing anything else.

  4. Choose one job

    Read fault codes, clear the displayed codes, or set the next service distance and date. The app asks for confirmation before a change.

  5. Verify on the dashboard

    After a reset, check the service date and distance on the motorcycle. After clearing codes, read them again.

Why use this app?

  • Focused: it resets the service reminder and reads or clears DTCs. It does not tune, flash, remap, or code the ECU.
  • No recurring cost: the app has no account, subscription, advertising, analytics, or paid feature unlock.
  • Inspectable: the complete application source and motorcycle profiles are published on GitHub under a noncommercial source-available license.
  • Cautious writes: the app checks the expected adapter and motorcycle response, asks for confirmation, sends a write once, and does not automatically retry an unclear result.

Download and installation

Android uses an APK from the project’s GitHub Releases page. iPhone uses the public Xcode project and your own Apple Account; a free iPhone installation lasts seven days and can then be reinstalled.
