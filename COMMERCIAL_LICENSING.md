# Commercial licensing

The public repository is source-available under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That license permits inspection,
personal study, modification, and redistribution only for purposes it defines
as permitted. It does not grant commercial-use rights.

Commercial use requires a separate written agreement with Dmytro Rodin before
the use begins. Depending on the facts, that can include:

- selling the app or a modified build;
- publishing a monetized, ad-supported, sponsored, or subscription build;
- bundling the software with paid hardware or services;
- using it as part of a paid workshop, dealer, fleet, or support service;
- developing or distributing it for the benefit of a commercial organization.

This list explains the project's licensing position; it does not replace or
modify the terms in `LICENSE`. Ask before proceeding if a proposed use has both
commercial and noncommercial aspects.

Donations, sponsorship, source availability, a fork, or a code contribution do
not themselves grant commercial rights. A publisher legally separate from
Dmytro Rodin—including a relative's company or sole proprietorship—must have a
separate written agreement that covers the relevant copyrights, distribution
channels, branding, revenue, support, and liability allocation before publishing
an official paid build.

The project names, logos, and store-listing assets are not included in any
third-party commercial license unless the written agreement expressly says so.
No one may present a fork as the official app or imply endorsement.

No public commercial-license template or contact address is offered yet. Add a
maintainer-controlled contact method and obtain appropriate legal and tax advice
before accepting a commercial license or appointing a publisher.
