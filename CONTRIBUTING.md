# Contributing

Testing reports and issue discussions are welcome once a public issue tracker is
available. Remove device identifiers, private captures, account data, and
third-party confidential or proprietary material before sharing anything.

The project is not currently accepting external code, documentation, artwork,
maps, or other copyrightable contributions. Do not open or merge a contribution
until Dmytro Rodin has put an appropriate written contributor agreement in place
and the contributor has signed it.

That agreement must confirm that the contributor has the right to submit the
work and give Dmytro Rodin the copyright ownership or sufficiently broad,
irrevocable relicensing rights needed to maintain both the noncommercial public
license and separate commercial licensing. A statement in an issue, a pull
request, a commit sign-off, or a donation is not a substitute for that agreement.

This temporary policy protects the project's ability to offer commercial
licenses later. A reviewed contributor agreement and submission process can be
added if external contributions become necessary.
